Tolagnaro

Fort-Dauphin is a city on the southeast coast of Madagascar. It is the capital of the Anosy Region and of the Taolagnaro District. It has been a port of local importance since the early 1500s. A new port, the Ehoala Port was built in 2006–2009. Fort-Dauphin was the first French settlement in Madagascar.

Fort-Dauphin serves as the capital of the Anosy Region and the Taolagnaro District in southeastern Madagascar. Historically, it has functioned as a significant port on the coast, with infrastructure improvements including the Port d'Ehola built between 2006 and 2009. The settlement maintains a population of approximately 61,008 and is situated in a tropical rainforest climate. The local economy is centered around its role as a regional administrative and port center.
