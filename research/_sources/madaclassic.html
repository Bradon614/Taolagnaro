Where Exploration Meets Preservation

Madagascar Classic Collection was founded in 2007 with the opening of Mandrare River Camp, the country’s first high-standard all-inclusive camp. Born from a passion for discovering Madagascar’s most remote and extraordinary landscapes, the Collection continues to open up new regions while treading lightly in fragile environments rich in traditional cultures. Through fully solar-powered camps, close collaboration with local communities and NGOs, and ongoing investment in conservation and community projects, Madagascar Classic Collection creates meaningful journeys that protect natural ecosystems, support local livelihoods, and preserve the unique heritage of Madagascar for generations to come.

Specialised Madagascar Travel

Madagascar Classic Collection is a specialist luxury travel company and ground handler dedicated to showcasing Madagascar’s most extraordinary landscapes, wildlife, and cultures. Established in 2007 with the opening of Mandrare River Camp, Madagascar’s first all-inclusive luxury tented camp, MCC combines the development and operation of exceptional camps with expert ground handling for tailor-made FIT and small group journeys. Our Camps

Mandrare River Camp

The Camp is situated on the banks of the Mandrare River, nestled under the shade of Tamarind Trees. The River is the lifeblood of the region and sustains the close by Antandroy village of Ifotaka and its sacred ancestral forests. The Spiny Forests found around the camp are unique to the Southern region of Madagascar and forms an important part of this region’s ecosystem and the wildlife adapted to this environment.

Namoroka Tsingy Camp

Namoroka National Park, a captivating natural wonder in northwestern Madagascar, showcases razor-sharp limestone formations called Tsingy. The park offers an awe-inspiring landscape, with narrow canyons, vertical spires, lush forests and unique flora. The otherworldly beauty of Namoroka Tsingy exemplifies Madagascar’s remarkable and diverse natural heritage, drawing in fascinated adventurers and nature enthusiasts alike.

Manafiafy Forest Lodge

Sparkling blue seas, verdant rainforests, abundant wildlife, distant mountains , this small and intimate lodge offers a taste of serenity. Nestled into a forest cover, luxury thatched bungalows offer private decked terraces inches from the sea, perfectly reflecting the tranquility of their natural surroundings.

Seasons of Madagascar

Madagascar has two main seasons that strongly influence travel:


A hot, humid rainy season from November to April, when the island is lush and green. Between January to March, expect heavy rainfall and occasional cyclones which can cause accessibility issues, including flooded or muddy roads and limited access to remote regions. Many lodges and National Parks are closed during this period.


And a cooler, drier season from May to October, which offers more reliable road conditions, easier travel between regions, and is generally considered the best time to visit for wildlife viewing, outdoor activities, and overall comfort.

The Ultimate Madagascar Experience:

Luxury Madagascar Itinerary | Andasibe, Mandrare River Camp, Manafiafy Forest Lodge & Namoroka Tsingy Camp
  • Day 1. Arrive and overnight Tana
  • Days 2 – 3. Transfer to Andasibe. Rainforest activites (2 nights)
  • Day 4. Transfer back to Tana. Overnight Tana
  • Days 5 – 7. Scheduled charter to Mandrare River Camp (3 nights)
  • Days 8 – 11, Scheduled charter to Manafiafy Forest Lodge (4 nights)
  • Day 12. Transfer back to Tana. Overnight Tana
  • Day 13 – 17. Namoroka Tsingy Camp (4 nights)

Highlights of this itinerary:

Andasibe-Mantadia National Park (Eastern Rainforest)

  • Explore Madagascar’s most accessible and wildlife-rich rainforest reserve
  • Encounter the famous Indri Indri lemur, along with diverse endemic species
  • Guided day and night walks in a biodiversity hotspot known for Madagascar wildlife tours
  • Ideal introduction to Madagascar’s unique ecosystems and rainforest experiences

Mandrare River Camp (Southern Madagascar – Spiny Forest & Culture)

  • Discover the remote south on a luxury Madagascar safari in the dramatic spiny forest landscape
  • Walk among ancient baobab trees and iconic ring-tailed lemursAuthentic cultural encounters with the Antandroy community
  • Exclusive access via scheduled charter flights, ensuring a secluded, off-the-beaten-track experience

Manafiafy Forest Lodge (South-East Coast – Rainforest & Ocean)

  • Stay in a pristine coastal reserve combining rainforest, mangroves, and Indian Ocean beaches
  • Experience eco-luxury travel in Madagascar with exceptional biodiversity
  • Activities available snorkelling, kayaking, forest walks, and seasonal whale watching and deepsea fishing.
  • One of Madagascar’s most unique destinations for beach and rainforest combinations

Namoroka Tsingy Camp (North-West Madagascar – Tsingy & Wilderness)

  • Explore the extraordinary tsingy limestone formations of Namoroka National Park
  • Discover caves, canyons, and rare endemic wildlife in a remote, untouched region
  • A true Madagascar adventure travel highlight, far from traditional tourist routes
  • Exclusive camp offering access to one of the least-visited tsingy landscapes in Madagascar

Madagascar’s Extraordinary Landscapes: Rainforest, Tsingy & Coastal Wilderness

Luxury Madagascar Itinerary: Masoala Forest Lodge, Namoroka Tsingy Camp and Anjajavy Lodge (or similar)

  • Day 1. Arrive and overnight Tana
  • Days 2 – 4. Private charter to Masoala Forest Lodge (3 nights)
  • Days 5 – 8. Private charter to Namoroka Tsingy Camp (4 nights)
  • Days 9 – 11. Private charter to Anjajavy Lodge (3 nights)

Highlights of this itinerary:

Masoala Forest Lodge (North-East Madagascar – Primary Rainforest & Marine Reserve)

  • Immerse in the untouched Masoala National Park, Madagascar’s largest protected rainforest
  • Stay at exclusive Masoala Forest Lodge, offering barefoot luxury in a pristine wilderness setting
  • Exceptional wildlife encounters including red ruffed lemurs, chameleons, and rare bird species
  • Unique combination of rainforest and marine experiences, with kayaking, snorkelling, and coastal exploration
  • A flagship destination for Madagascar eco-luxury travel and biodiversity-focused safaris

Namoroka Tsingy Camp (North-West Madagascar – Remote Tsingy Landscapes)

  • Discover dramatic tsingy limestone formations, caves, and canyons in Namoroka National Park
  • One of the most remote and least-visited parks, ideal for exclusive Madagascar adventure travel
  • Guided explorations through a surreal landscape rich in endemic wildlife
  • Stay at intimate Namoroka Tsingy Camp, offering access to a truly off-the-beaten-path experience
  • Perfect for travellers seeking unique geological formations and untouched wilderness

Anjajavy Lodge (North-West Coast – Private Reserve & Coastal Forest)

  • Experience refined luxury at Anjajavy Lodge, set within a private coastal reserve
  • Explore rare tsingy outcrops, dry deciduous forest, and pristine beaches
  • High density of wildlife including Coquerel’s sifaka, brown lemurs, and diverse birdlife
  • Seamless blend of luxury Madagascar safari and beach escape
  • One of Madagascar’s most iconic properties for exclusive, all-inclusive nature experiences

Madagascar’s Ultra Luxury Wild Contrasts: Coastal Forest Reserve and Island Escape

Luxury Madagascar Itinerary | Anjajavy | Miavana by Time + Tide. Anjajavy, Miavana & Northern Helicopter Safaris.

  • Day 1. Arrive and overnight Tana
  • Days 2 – 3. Activities at Anjajavy Lodge (3 nights)
  • Day 4. Private charter flight to Diego Suarez, from there Private Helicopter flight to Miavana overnight Miavana
  • Days 5 – 7. Activities at Miavana (3 nights)
  • Day 8. Private Helicopter flight to Nosy Be Airport for International flight home.

Highlights of this itinerary:

Anjajavy Lodge (North-West Madagascar – Private Reserve & Coastal Wilderness)

  • Stay within a protected private nature reserve in Madagascar, combining dry deciduous forest, tsingy formations, and pristine coastline
  • Exceptional wildlife encounters including Coquerel’s sifaka, lemurs, and endemic bird species in a highly biodiverse ecosystem
  • Explore dramatic tsingy outcrops, baobabs, and mangrove channels in one of Madagascar’s most exclusive conservation areas
  • A seamless blend of luxury Madagascar safari and beach escape, ideal for immersive yet refined travel

Miavana Private Island (North-East Madagascar – Ultra-Luxury Island Escape)

  • Arrive by scenic helicopter transfer to one of the most exclusive private island resorts in Madagascar
  • Stay on a remote island sanctuary offering barefoot luxury, privacy, and world-class service
  • Enjoy a wide range of experiences including marine adventures, lemur trekking, and helicopter excursions
  • A standout destination for ultra-luxury Madagascar travel, combining wilderness with five-star island living

Helicopter Safari: Daraina Region (Northern Madagascar – Remote Wilderness)

  • Exclusive helicopter tour over the Daraina region, one of Madagascar’s most remote and least-visited landscapes
  • Discover rare ecosystems and endemic wildlife, including habitats of the critically endangered golden-crowned sifaka
  • A unique aerial perspective showcasing Madagascar’s untouched forests and rugged terrain
  • A highlight for travellers seeking off-the-grid Madagascar adventure experiences

Helicopter Exploration: Ankarana Reserve (Tsingy & Caves)

  • Soar above the dramatic Ankarana Tsingy formations, a spectacular limestone “stone forest” landscape
  • Discover hidden caves, underground rivers, and canyons within this unique geological reserve
  • One of the best ways to experience Madagascar’s iconic tsingy formations from above and on the ground
  • Combines adventure, geology, and exclusivity in a true Madagascar helicopter safari experience

Scenic Helicopter Flight to Nosy Be (Northern Coastline & Islands)

  • Conclude with a breathtaking helicopter flight across Madagascar’s northern coastline
  • Fly over turquoise waters, remote islands, coral reefs, and baobab-dotted landscapes
  • Seamless and luxurious transfer showcasing the diversity of Madagascar’s coastal and marine environments
  • A signature experience for high-end Madagascar itineraries and private charter travel
