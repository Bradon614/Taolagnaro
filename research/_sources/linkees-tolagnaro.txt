Read more ↓ Fort-Dauphin serves as the capital of the Anosy Region and the Taolagnaro District in southeastern Madagascar. Historically, it has functioned as a significant port on the coast, with infrastructure improvements including the Port d'Ehola built between 2006 and 2009. The settlement maintains a population of approximately 61,008 and is situated in a tropical rainforest climate.
